# Catch Xavier!

Original title: **抓到澤維爾！**

**[▶️ Play Now](https://althea2025.github.io/catch-xavier/)**

One quiet afternoon. One very alert man. One hug to steal. ✨

## About

Angela has a simple plan: sneak up on Xavier and hug him from behind before he notices her. Unfortunately, he is exceptionally hard to surprise.

Watch him move through the study, slip behind furniture, keep your footsteps quiet, and use a little magic to draw his attention elsewhere. You are only two steps away—can you reach him before he turns around?

A single-player **2D stealth romance mini-game** with an otome-inspired atmosphere: warm, playful, and just a little mischievous. Made for short sessions and that irresistible urge to try again.

## Gameplay Features

- **Watch and weave:** Xavier reads, moves, pauses, and looks around. Use furniture to break his line of sight and find a way closer.
- **Mind your footsteps:** Walk, sneak, or sprint; different floor surfaces also affect how much noise you make.
- **Magic distraction:** Make a nearby object sound off and draw him over to investigate. Repeated strange noises may make him suspicious.
- **Those final steps:** Even behind him, getting too close can give you away. Be ready when the hug prompt appears!
- **One more try:** Multiple difficulties and outcome scenes, desktop and mobile/tablet controls, and progress saved in your browser.

## Controls

### Desktop

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | WASD / arrow keys, or click the floor to move |
| Sneak | C to toggle on / off |
| Sprint | Hold Shift; release to return to your previous movement mode |
| Hug | E when the prompt appears |
| Magic distraction | Q, or the on-screen magic button |
| Choose a distraction target | Click an object, or Tab to cycle through nearby objects |
| Pause / resume | Esc, or the on-screen buttons |
| Retry | R during an ending, or the retry button on the result screen |
| Sound | Toggle in the upper-right corner |

### Mobile & Tablet

Hold a direction button to move. Tap Sneak to toggle it, or hold Sprint for a temporary burst of speed; releasing it restores your previous mode. Direction and action buttons can be used together.

Tap Hug when close enough, and use the HUD’s magic button to cast a distraction. You can also tap the floor to move and tap objects to select a target. Pause and sound controls are at the top of the screen.

## Platforms

Play directly in a desktop, phone, or tablet browser using the link above. No game download, Node installation, or additional runtime is needed.

Enable sound in the upper-right corner to hear music and effects. If your device stays silent, check its volume and silent mode too. Progress is stored in your current browser; clearing site data may reset it.

## Technology

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Canvas 2D, and Web Audio. Hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages, with localStorage for browser-local progress.
